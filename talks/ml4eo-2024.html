ML4EO 2024 — Machine Learning for Earth Observation

Attended ML4EO 2024 at the University of Exeter — a dedicated conference on Machine Learning for Earth Observation, bringing together researchers and practitioners applying ML techniques to satellite and geospatial data for environmental and humanitarian challenges. The event featured 40+ oral presentations, poster sessions, keynotes, and workshops covering topics from forest monitoring and disaster response to agricultural assessment and marine conservation.

Co-authored an academic poster submission with Anna Zanchetta (Humanitarian OpenStreetMap Team), contributing work at the intersection of AI-assisted mapping and Earth observation. The submission focused on HOT's fAIr approach to community-led AI model training for building detection from satellite imagery.
