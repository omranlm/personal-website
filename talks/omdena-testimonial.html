Omdena × HOT fAIr Innovation Challenge

Gave a client testimonial video as HOT's Senior Technical Product Owner for AI, speaking about Omdena's contribution to the fAIr AI innovation challenge. The testimonial covered the value of the Omdena collaboration model — where a global community of volunteer data scientists works on a real-world humanitarian problem in a short sprint — and how the resulting AI models and workflows were integrated into HOT's fAIr platform.

The collaboration produced working ML models for building detection from satellite imagery, directly enabling the open-source fAIr service that is now in production use by the humanitarian mapping community.
