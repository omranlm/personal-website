Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Overview

This site is a personal portfolio with no user accounts, no logins, and no forms that collect personal information for marketing purposes. The only data processed is a small, anonymous set of usage statistics gathered through Google Analytics, described in full below — and you control whether that happens at all.

What We Collect

When analytics is enabled, Google Analytics 4 records anonymous, aggregated information such as:

  • Which pages are viewed and for how long
  • Approximate location (country/city level, derived from IP — the IP address itself is not stored)
  • Device, browser, and screen type
  • How you arrived at the site (e.g. search engine, direct link, social media)

We deliberately do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or any other personally identifying information
  • Your full IP address
  • Any data used for advertising or cross-site tracking

Analytics data stays within Google Analytics and is never sold, shared with other third parties, or combined with data from other sites.

Cookies We Use

Cookies are only set if you accept analytics below (or via the banner shown on your first visit). We use exactly two:

Distinguishes unique visitors · expires after 2 years
Persists session state for this GA4 property · expires after 2 years

No advertising cookies, no social-media tracking pixels, and no third-party cookies are ever set by this site.

Changes to This Policy

If what this site collects ever changes, this page will be updated to reflect it. Checking back periodically is the only way to stay informed, as no notifications are sent.
